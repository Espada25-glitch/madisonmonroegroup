John brings more than 15 years of experience across business ownership, financial services, agency leadership, and customer strategy. He has built a business, led multidisciplinary teams, and helped translate complex commercial priorities into action.

His experience includes a vice president role at Synchrony, senior client leadership at GlueIQ, sales and marketing leadership at Troon Privé, and ownership of a State Farm agency.

Alongside his commercial work, John teaches at Georgetown McDonough, NYU Stern, and UVA Darden. He is a Research Fellow with the NRF Business of Retail Initiative at Georgetown and holds an MS in Management from the University of Miami and a BS from Florida International University.
