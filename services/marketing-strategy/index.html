The work

From customer evidence to marketing priorities.

01

Understand the decision

Review customer and sales evidence, current messaging, channel performance, and constraints. Agree the business question before choosing research methods.

02

Choose a direction

Develop or refine customer segments and positioning. Test assumptions against available evidence and identify where additional customer research is needed.

What you take forward

Clear choices. Useful deliverables.

Depending on the agreed scope, deliverables may include:

  • A customer insight and segmentation brief, including priority audiences and their buying criteria.
  • A positioning and messaging framework with supporting evidence.
  • A go-to-market plan linking the offer, customer journey, and priority channels.
  • A ranked marketing investment plan with assumptions and trade-offs.
  • A practical roadmap with owners, milestones, and measures of progress.

John leads the strategy and client relationship. Scope, timing, responsibilities, and fees are agreed before work begins; independent specialists may contribute when the agreed work calls for additional expertise.

Research into practice

Make your expertise easier to understand—and verify.

John coauthored How to Get AI to Surface Your Brand with Luca Cian and Luc Wathieu in Harvard Business Review. The research examines how AI systems surface retail brands, emphasizing clear identity, meaningful attributes, and credible evidence.

For a services business, a practical application is to explain the buyer’s problem, show how the service addresses it, and support claims with verifiable expertise. This is an application of the research, not a promise of AI visibility or a tested causal result for consulting firms.

How to choose a marketing strategy consultant ↗

Questions before you begin.

Is this suitable for a founder-led B2B business?

Yes, when the central question concerns customers, positioning, go-to-market choices, or investment priorities. A focused engagement can address one consequential decision. The first conversation establishes fit.

How do you approach customer insight and segmentation?

Begin with the decision and available evidence, including customer conversations, sales patterns, and market information. Define useful differences in customer needs and buying behavior, then identify the segments the business can serve credibly. Research methods are agreed within scope.

Does this include campaign execution?

The core engagement covers strategy, priorities, and an actionable plan. Any additional execution support, specialist involvement, or handoff to an existing agency is defined separately in the agreed scope.

How is marketing strategy different from growth strategy?

Marketing strategy focuses on whom to reach, how to position the offer, and where to invest marketing effort. Growth strategy evaluates where the business should expand, including markets, services, and diversification.

What does an engagement cost?

Fees and timing depend on the question, evidence required, and deliverables. These are agreed before work begins.
